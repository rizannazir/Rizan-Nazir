About

No film school. No design classroom. Just curiosity and a lot of tabs open.

Rizan taught himself design and editing off YouTube, one tutorial at a time — no formal training, no shortcuts. That self-taught habit is the thread through everything since: gaming logos as a kid, freelance graphic design, then video, then two years inside his college's IEDC — the startup and innovation cell — working his way up from Assistant CCO to CEO's Executive Assistant while freelancing on the side. Rayo Productions was born in the middle of that, in October 2024, out of one idea he'd proven on himself first: raw, honest content beats over-produced content, every time.

Thinking

Real is the new growth hack.

Rizan's own content — unscripted, talking-head, no engineered hooks — has out-performed nearly everything produced. That's not a slogan he sells clients; it's what he found out testing it on himself, before it became how Rayo works.

He's also open about the harder question underneath it: being pulled in multiple creative directions at once, and wondering whether versatility itself — not one fixed lane — is the actual career.
